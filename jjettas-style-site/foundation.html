Programs

Three ways we show up

fdn-camp

Summer Camps

Free two-day camps every summer — fundamentals, teamwork, and a whole lot of Griddy. Every kid leaves with cleats, a backpack, and a memory.

fdn-drive

Holiday Drives

Back-to-school backpacks in August, turkeys in November, toys in December. The calendar never skips a season of giving.

Community Days

Field cleanups, school visits, and open practices — showing up in person, not just in the headlines.
