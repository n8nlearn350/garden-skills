Privacy Policy

Last updated · January 2026

1. What we collect

This site itself collects nothing automatically. If you contact us — via the partnership form or email — we receive the details you choose to share (name, brand, email, message) so we can reply.

2. Photo edit mode

The "Edit photos" feature stores your replacement images in your own browser's local storage. Those images never leave your device and are never sent to any server.

3. What we don't do

We don't sell personal information, run cross-site trackers, or share your details with advertisers. Analytics, if any, are aggregate and anonymous.

4. Your rights

Ask us to review, correct, or delete anything you've sent us — email team@example.com and we'll handle it promptly.

5. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and note the change here for 30 days.
